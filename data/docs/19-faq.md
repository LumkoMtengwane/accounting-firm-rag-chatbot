# Frequently Asked Questions

Document status: current. Last updated: 2 February 2026.

## Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. The first 30-minute introductory call is free. After that, advisory time is charged at the rates in the fee schedule.

## Can you give me tax advice in this chat?

No. This chat gives general information from our documents. Personal tax advice needs a consultant who has reviewed your situation.

## How long does it take to get my tax return done?

Usually 5 to 10 working days from receiving all documents. In July to October it can take longer.

## Can I pay in instalments?

For annual financial statements we invoice 50% on acceptance and 50% on delivery. For other services, speak to the managing partner about a payment plan before work begins.

## Do you work with clients outside Gauteng?

Yes. We work remotely with clients anywhere in South Africa. Meetings are held on video call.

## Can you register my company?

Yes. We register companies with CIPC and can also register you for tax, VAT and UIF afterwards.

## What happens if I have not filed returns for several years?

We can help you get compliant. We review your position, apply for relief where SARS allows it and file the outstanding returns. Fees for catch-up work are quoted after we see your documents.

## Which accounting software do you use?

We use a cloud accounting package, named in your engagement letter. You get read-only access.

## Do you handle crypto or foreign income?

We can include foreign income in your return. For crypto, a consultant must first review your records. Book an advisory session.
