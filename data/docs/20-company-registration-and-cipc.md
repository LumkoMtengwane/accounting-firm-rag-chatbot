# Company Registration and CIPC Annual Returns

Document status: current. Last updated: 12 January 2026.

## Registering a company

We register private companies (Pty) Ltd with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC).

## What we need

- Three proposed company names, in order of preference
- ID copy and proof of address for each director and shareholder
- Details of the shareholding split
- Registered address for the company
- Whether you want us to register the company for tax, VAT, PAYE or UIF afterwards

## Timeline

CIPC normally registers a company within a few working days after a complete application. We send you the registration certificate and the company documents.

## After registration

- Open a company bank account. We provide a letter to help the bank.
- Register for tax and, if needed, VAT, PAYE and UIF.
- Keep a company register and minutes.

## Annual returns

Every company must submit a CIPC annual return each year after its registration anniversary. Missing it can lead to deregistration. We offer a CIPC annual return service. See the fee schedule.

## Beneficial ownership

CIPC requires companies to file information on beneficial owners. We collect the details from you and file them with the annual return or when ownership changes.

## What we do not do

We do not give legal advice on shareholder agreements. We can recommend an attorney.
