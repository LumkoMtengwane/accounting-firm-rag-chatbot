# SARS Correspondence, Verifications and Audits

Document status: current. Last updated: 2 February 2026.

## If you receive a SARS letter or SMS

Forward it to us within 2 working days. SARS letters have response deadlines, often 21 business days, and the clock starts when SARS sends the letter, not when you read it.

## What we do

1. Log the letter and note the response deadline.
2. Tell you what SARS is asking for.
3. Collect the documents SARS requires.
4. Prepare the response and send you a copy for approval.
5. Submit the response on eFiling.

## Types of SARS requests

- Verification: SARS asks you to support figures in a return. Usually a document request.
- Audit: a deeper review of a tax period. May involve interviews and large document requests.
- Correction or revised assessment: SARS changes your assessment. We check whether it is correct and whether to dispute it.

## Fees

Responding to SARS letters on work we prepared is charged at advisory rates after the first hour. If the letter relates to a period before our engagement, all time is charged at advisory rates. We will estimate the time before we start.

## What we cannot do

We cannot stop SARS from auditing you, promise an outcome or guarantee that a dispute will succeed. We never ignore a letter. If an audit becomes a legal dispute, we may recommend a tax lawyer.
