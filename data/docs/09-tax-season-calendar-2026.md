## SARS dates we plan around

- Individual filing season opens in July and closes in October.
- EMP201 is due by the 7th of the following month.
- VAT201 is due on the date shown on your SARS profile.
- Provisional tax payments fall at the end of August and the end of February.

## Busy periods

July to October is our busiest time. Turnaround for individual returns can stretch from 5 to 10 working days. Book early.

## Tell us if

You receive an automated SARS notice such as a verification request, a correction notice or a letter of audit. Follow the SARS correspondence process in our SARS document.
