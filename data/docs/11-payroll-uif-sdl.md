# Payroll, UIF and SDL

Document status: current. Last updated: 12 January 2026.

## What our payroll service covers

- Monthly payslips for each employee
- PAYE, UIF and SDL calculations
- EMP201 monthly submission and payment reference
- UIF declarations
- Bi-annual EMP501 reconciliation and IRP5 certificates
- Leave records, if you ask us to keep them

## What you send us

By the 3rd of the following month send:

- New starters, with ID copy, bank details, tax number and start date
- Leavers, with last working day and leave balance
- Changes to salaries, allowances or deductions
- Overtime, bonuses and unpaid leave for the month

## Timeline

1. We prepare payslips and send you a payroll summary for approval.
2. After you approve, we issue payslips and the EMP201.
3. You pay SARS using the reference we give you. SARS expects the EMP201 and payment by the 7th.

## What we need from you before the first run

- Company payroll registration details with SARS (PAYE, UIF and SDL reference numbers)
- Employment contracts for all employees
- A completed starter form for each employee
