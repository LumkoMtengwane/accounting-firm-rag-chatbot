# Client Onboarding and FICA Verification

Document status: current. Last updated: 12 January 2026.

## Why we verify you

As an accountable institution we must identify and verify every client under the Financial Intelligence Centre Act (FICA) before we start work. This protects you and us from fraud.

## Documents for individuals

- Certified or original South African ID, or passport for foreign nationals
- Proof of residential address, not older than 3 months (utility bill, bank statement or municipal account)
- SARS tax number and eFiling login details, if you already have them
- Bank confirmation letter showing account holder name and account number

## Documents for sole proprietors

All individual documents, plus:

- Business name and trading address
- Business bank account confirmation letter
- VAT registration certificate, if registered

## Documents for companies

- CIPC registration certificate (CoR14.3) and the latest CIPC annual return
- Directors' IDs and proof of address for each director
- Shareholder register
- Proof of business address
- Resolution authorising us to act for the company
- Bank confirmation letter in the company name

## How long onboarding takes

Once we have every document, verification takes up to 3 working days. Missing or unclear documents delay the start date.

## What happens after verification

We send you the engagement letter and set up your client file. You will be asked to sign a SARS power of attorney or eFiling authority so that we can work on your SARS profile.
