# About Marula Ridge Accountants

Document status: current. Last updated: 2 February 2026.
Note: Marula Ridge Accountants is a fictional firm created for a software demonstration.

## Who we are

Marula Ridge Accountants (Pty) Ltd is a small accounting and tax practice in Centurion, Gauteng. We serve individuals, sole proprietors and small companies. We have been in practice since 2014 and have a team of eleven people.

## What we do

- Personal income tax returns and SARS eFiling support
- Bookkeeping and monthly management reports for small businesses
- Annual financial statements and company tax returns
- VAT registration, VAT201 returns and VAT reviews
- Payroll, UIF, SDL and EMP201 submissions
- Company registration and CIPC annual returns
- Advisory sessions on tax planning and business structure

## What we do not do

- We do not audit financial statements. We can refer you to a registered auditor.
- We do not give legal advice or investment advice.
- We do not submit anything to SARS on your behalf until you have approved the figures in writing.
