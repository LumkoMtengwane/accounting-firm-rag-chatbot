# Document Storage and Secure Upload

Document status: current. Last updated: 5 January 2026.

## How to send us documents

Use the secure client portal. You receive a personal link when your onboarding is complete. You can upload PDFs and photos up to 25 MB each.

## What not to do

- Do not email ID copies, bank statements or passwords in plain email.
- Do not share your SARS eFiling password with anyone. We work on your profile through an authority that you give us, not with your password.
- Do not send documents through social media or messaging apps.

## If the portal does not work

Call the front desk. We will arrange an alternative. If you must email a document, we will send you a password-protected request and you can protect the file with a password that you share by phone.

## How long we keep documents

Tax and accounting records are kept for at least 5 years after the work is finished. Original paper documents are returned within 14 days of a written request.

## Who can see your documents

Only staff working on your file, and our cloud storage provider under a confidentiality agreement.

## Lost or stolen documents

Tell us immediately if you believe your documents, ID or SARS login have been compromised. We will advise you on steps to protect your SARS profile.
