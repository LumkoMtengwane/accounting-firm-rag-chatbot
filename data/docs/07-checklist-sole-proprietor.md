# Document Checklist: Sole Proprietor

Document status: current. Last updated: 15 June 2026.

## Income records

- Sales invoices issued during the tax year
- Bank statements for every business account, all 12 months
- Card machine or payment platform statements
- Cash takings records, if you accept cash

## Expense records

- Supplier invoices and till slips for business expenses
- Rent, utilities, telephone and internet bills for the business
- Vehicle expenses: logbook, fuel slips and service invoices if you claim business travel
- Insurance, licences and subscriptions
- Wages and contractor payments

## Balance sheet items

- Stock count at year end, if you hold stock
- List of money owed to you (debtors) and money you owe (creditors)
- Loan statements and the interest charged
- Asset purchases above R7,000 with invoices

## Tax records

- Provisional tax payment confirmations (first and second periods)
- VAT201 returns and proof of payment, if you are VAT registered
- Previous year's financial statements and assessment

## What you receive from us

Simple annual financial statements, your tax return for approval and a short summary of what you owe or are owed.
