# Services and Fees Schedule 2026

Document status: current. Effective from 1 March 2026. This schedule replaces the 2025 schedule.
All amounts are in rand and exclude VAT unless stated.

## Personal tax returns

- Individual tax return, one IRP5 and no other income: R950
- Individual tax return with rental income, a provisional taxpayer profile or foreign income: R1,650
- Late submission clean-up (a return more than one year overdue): quoted separately

## Sole proprietors

- Sole proprietor tax return with simple financial statements: R2,900

## Companies

- Annual financial statements and company tax return, small company: from R6,500
- Dormant company (no transactions in the year): R3,200
- Company registration with CIPC: R1,950 plus CIPC fees
- CIPC annual return: R650

## Monthly bookkeeping

| Package | Transactions per month | Fee per month |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | up to 50 | R1,450 |
| Standard | up to 150 | R2,650 |
| Plus | up to 300 | R4,200 |

Transactions above the package limit are billed at R12 each.

## VAT and payroll

- VAT201 return preparation and submission: R650 per return
- VAT registration with SARS: R1,200
- Payroll processing: R180 per employee per month, with a minimum of R540 per month
- EMP201 submission: included in the payroll fee

## Advisory time

- Consultant: R850 per hour
- Partner: R1,300 per hour
- The first 30-minute introductory call is free.
