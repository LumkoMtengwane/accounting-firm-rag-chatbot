# Document Checklist: Company Annual Financial Statements

Document status: current. Last updated: 15 June 2026.

## Records we need

- Bank statements for all company accounts, for the full financial year
- Sales invoices and a debtors list at year end
- Supplier invoices and a creditors list at year end
- Payroll records: payslips, EMP201 and EMP501 reconciliation, IRP5 summary
- VAT201 returns and proof of payment
- Loan agreements and statements, including director and shareholder loans
- Fixed asset register with purchases and disposals during the year
- Stock count sheets, if the company holds stock

## Company records

- CIPC annual return and the latest registration certificate
- Minutes of directors' and shareholders' meetings
- Share register and any share transfers
- Changes to directors or the registered address

## Our process

1. You send documents by the deadline in our late-documents policy.
2. We capture and reconcile the records, and send you queries.
3. We prepare draft annual financial statements.
4. The directors review and approve them in writing.
5. We prepare and submit the company tax return (ITR14) to SARS.

## How long it takes

About 6 weeks after we receive a complete set of documents. Incomplete records add time.
