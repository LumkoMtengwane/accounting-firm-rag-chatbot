# Privacy Notice (POPIA)

Document status: current. Last updated: 5 January 2026.

## Who is responsible

Marula Ridge Accountants (Pty) Ltd is the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Our information officer is Thandi Mokoena.

## What personal information we collect

Identity numbers, contact details, addresses, tax numbers, bank details, income and expense records, employee details for payroll clients and any other information you give us to do the work.

## Why we collect it

To prepare returns and financial statements, to submit information to SARS and CIPC, to meet FICA obligations and to invoice you. We do not use your information for marketing without your consent.

## Who we share it with

SARS, CIPC, our cloud accounting and storage providers, and regulators when the law requires it. We do not sell your information.

## How we protect it

- Documents are stored in encrypted cloud storage with access limited to staff who need them.
- We ask clients to upload documents through our secure portal and not by plain email.
- Staff sign confidentiality agreements.

## How long we keep it

We keep tax and accounting records for at least 5 years after the work is finished, as the law requires. After that we securely delete or destroy them unless the law requires longer retention.

## Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, subject to our legal duty to keep records. Ask the information officer in writing. If you are unhappy you may complain to the Information Regulator.
