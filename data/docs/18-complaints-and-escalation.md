# Complaints and Escalation

Document status: current. Last updated: 5 January 2026.

## How to complain

Tell us in writing (email is fine) what went wrong and what you would like us to do. Send it to your consultant. If you prefer, send it directly to the managing partner, Thandi Mokoena.

## What happens next

1. We acknowledge your complaint within 2 working days.
2. A partner who was not involved in the work reviews it.
3. We reply with findings and proposed action within 10 working days.
4. If we made a mistake, we fix it and, where fair, adjust the fee.

## If you are still unhappy

You may escalate to the managing partner. If we cannot resolve the complaint, you may approach the professional body the relevant accountant belongs to. We will give you the details on request.

## Errors on returns

If a return has an error, tell us immediately. We will check it and, where needed, submit a correction to SARS. Penalties caused by our error are ours to deal with. Penalties caused by incorrect information you gave us are yours.

## Urgent problems

For an urgent SARS or payment problem, call the front desk and ask for a callback from the tax manager the same day.
