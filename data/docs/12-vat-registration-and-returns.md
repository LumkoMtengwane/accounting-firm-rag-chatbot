# VAT Registration and Returns

Document status: current. Last updated: 12 January 2026.
Reminder: VAT thresholds and rules are set by SARS and can change. We confirm the current position with you before acting.

## Do I have to register for VAT?

Registration is compulsory when your taxable turnover goes over the threshold set by SARS in a 12-month period. You may register voluntarily below the threshold if you meet SARS conditions. We do not decide for you. A consultant checks your turnover and advises you in an advisory session.

## What we need to register you

- Company or business registration documents
- ID and proof of address for the responsible person
- Business bank account confirmation letter
- Turnover records for the last 12 months
- A description of the business activities

## After registration

SARS assigns a VAT number and a VAT filing period (monthly or every two months). We submit the VAT201 for you.

## VAT201 process

1. You send sales and purchase invoices and bank statements by the 10th of the following month.
2. We prepare the return and send you a summary for approval.
3. You approve in writing.
4. We submit the VAT201 on eFiling.
5. You pay SARS the amount due using the payment reference we provide.

## Late documents

If we receive your documents after the cut-off, we may be unable to submit by the SARS due date. Penalties and interest charged by SARS are your responsibility.
