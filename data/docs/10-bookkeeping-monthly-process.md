# Monthly Bookkeeping Process

Document status: current. Last updated: 12 January 2026.

## What you send us each month

- Bank statements for all business accounts, by the 5th of the following month
- Sales invoices issued and cash takings records
- Supplier invoices and slips for expenses
- Payroll summary, if payroll is not run by us

## What we do

1. Capture every transaction and match it to a bank line.
2. Reconcile each bank account to the statement.
3. Query anything unclear. We list the queries in one email, not many.
4. Close the month and prepare a management report: income statement, balance sheet summary and cash position.
5. Send the report by the 15th of the month.

## Packages and limits

Fees depend on the package (Starter, Standard or Plus) in the current fee schedule. If your transactions regularly exceed your package limit, we will suggest moving up a package.

## Queries

If you do not answer queries within 5 working days, we will park the items in a suspense account so that the month can close. Parked items can distort your report until they are resolved.

## Software

We work in the cloud accounting software agreed in your engagement letter. You get read-only access so that you can see the books at any time.
