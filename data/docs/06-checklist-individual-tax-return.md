# Document Checklist: Individual Tax Return

Document status: current. Last updated: 15 June 2026.

## Always needed

- IRP5 or IT3(a) from every employer for the tax year
- Latest SARS notice of assessment, if you have one
- Medical aid tax certificate
- Retirement annuity (RA) tax certificate
- Interest and dividend certificates from banks and investment providers (IT3(b) and IT3(c))

## Needed if they apply to you

- Rental income: lease agreement, rent received per month and receipts for expenses (rates, repairs, agent fees)
- Home office claim: employer confirmation that you must work from home, floor area of the office and the home, and monthly costs
- Travel claim: a logbook for the whole year, the vehicle purchase price and the odometer readings at the start and end of the year
- Medical expenses paid out of pocket: invoices and proof of payment
- Donations: Section 18A receipts from the organisation
- Capital gains: sale agreements, purchase documents and costs of improvement
- Foreign income: statements and the exchange rate used

## What we do with them

We capture the figures, send you a summary to approve in writing, then submit through SARS eFiling. We send you the notice of assessment when SARS issues it.

## Tips

- Send documents as clear PDFs or photos where all four corners show.
- Name each file clearly, for example "IRP5 Employer Name 2026".
- Never send your eFiling password by email.
