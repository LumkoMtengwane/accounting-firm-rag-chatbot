# Late Documents Policy

Document status: current. Last updated: 2 February 2026.

## Why deadlines matter

SARS deadlines are fixed. If your documents reach us late we may not have time to prepare accurate work and meet the SARS due date.

## Our cut-off dates

The tax season calendar lists our internal cut-off for each service. If you miss a cut-off:

- We will still try to meet the SARS date, but we cannot promise it.
- Work outside the normal turnaround is treated as urgent and charged at 25% above the normal fee.
- If the SARS deadline is missed because documents were late, penalties and interest are your responsibility.

## Incomplete documents

We do not start a return until we have the documents on the relevant checklist. If something is missing we send one email listing what is outstanding. After 5 working days without a reply we send a reminder and pause the work.

## Extensions

If you cannot meet a cut-off, tell us as early as possible. We may be able to agree a new date if SARS allows it. We never promise an extension from SARS.

## Urgent work

Urgent work is accepted when capacity allows. A consultant will confirm the urgent fee and date in writing before we start.
