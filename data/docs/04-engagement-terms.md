# Engagement Terms

Document status: current. Last updated: 2 February 2026.

## When our engagement starts

Our engagement starts when you have signed the engagement letter and we have completed client verification (see the onboarding document). We do not start work or submit anything to SARS before both are done.

## Scope of work

The engagement letter lists exactly what we will do. Work outside that list is billed as advisory time at the rates in the fee schedule, and we will tell you before we start.

## Your responsibilities

- Give us complete and accurate information.
- Send documents by the deadlines in our late-documents policy.
- Tell us within 5 working days if your contact details, bank, address or business structure change.
- Approve figures in writing before we submit a return.

## Our responsibilities

- Prepare work with reasonable skill and care.
- Meet the internal deadlines we agree with you.
- Keep your information confidential.
- Tell you promptly if we find an error or a risk.

## Ending the engagement

Either side may end the engagement with 30 days written notice. Fees for work completed up to the end date remain payable. We return your original documents within 14 days of a written request.

## Limits of our service

We prepare returns from the information you give us. We do not verify the accuracy of source documents unless the engagement letter says so. Our answers to general questions are information, not personalised tax advice, until a consultant has reviewed your situation.
