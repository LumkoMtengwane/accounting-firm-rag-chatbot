# Payment Terms and Invoicing

Document status: current. Last updated: 2 February 2026.

## When we invoice

- Once-off work (tax returns, registrations): on delivery of the work for your approval.
- Monthly services (bookkeeping, payroll): on the first working day of the month, in advance.
- Annual financial statements: 50% on acceptance of the engagement and 50% on delivery.

## Payment terms

Invoices are payable within 14 days. We accept EFT only. We do not accept cash. Use your invoice number as the payment reference.

## Late payment

- After 14 days we send a reminder.
- After 30 days we may pause work and monthly services.
- We do not submit returns to SARS on accounts more than 60 days overdue, unless the SARS deadline would be missed. In that case a partner decides.
- Interest on overdue accounts is charged at 1.5% per month.

## Disputes

If you disagree with an invoice, tell us within 7 days of receiving it. We will review it and reply within 5 working days.

## VAT

Fees are quoted excluding VAT. VAT is added to invoices at the standard rate.

## Debit orders

Monthly clients can pay by debit order. Ask the front desk for the mandate.
